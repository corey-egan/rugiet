## Why this works

1. **Reply-generating:** The ask is genuine, easy (one line), and personal. It doesn't feel like a trick — it feels like a CEO who actually wants to know. Men in this demo respond to direct asks from real people, not gamified engagement tactics.
2. **Deliverability:** A reply to email 1 is the single strongest signal for Gmail/Outlook to move future sends to primary. The longevity launch review flagged this as a gap across all flows.
3. **Data capture:** The replies become a free segmentation signal. "Recovery" replies get different launch-day content than "body composition" replies. This is engagement data you can't get from a form.
4. **Brand-consistent:** Dinner-party-guy register. Direct, matter-of-fact, no hedging. The competitive positioning ("no gray-market vials") echoes the announcement email's three non-negotiables without repeating it verbatim.
5. **Plain text = personal:** No design, no logo, no CTA button. Reads like a note from a person, not a marketing email. This format consistently outperforms designed emails for reply rate.
