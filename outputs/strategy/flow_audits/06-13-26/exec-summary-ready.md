## Ready-specific standards

### Product descriptor

**Canonical line:** "3 medications. 5 benefits. 1 dose."

### Discount architecture

| Flow | Code | Discount |
|---|---|---|
| Browse | RUGIET10 | 10% |
| Welcome | 5XFAST | 15% |
| Cart | CART15 | 15% |
| Cart (last email only, if kept) | CART20 | 20% |

**Browse/Welcome overlap risk:** RUGIET10 (10%) and 5XFAST (15%) target overlapping audiences. Suppress browse sends for anyone active in the welcome flow, or unify the offer.

**Braze suppression (confirmed):** When a subscriber enters a higher-intent flow, they are removed from the prior flow. Codes are not overlapping.

### Banner architecture

Trust emails carry no coupon. Offer emails carry the banner. Body copy sells the product; the banner/CTA carries the offer.

### Trademark (per trademark-usage.md)

- Standalone "Ready" → ™ on first mention only, never ®
- "Rugiet Ready" (full product name) → ® on first mention only
- After first mention → no symbol needed
- "Performance Medicine For Men" → no symbol (pending, contested)
