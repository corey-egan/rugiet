Month 5 is where anecdotal NAD+ experiences live — properly as customer stories with provider commentary, not as clinical claims.

## NAD+-Specific Considerations

### Regulatory context
NMN was confirmed by the FDA in September 2025 as not excluded from the dietary supplement definition. Subsequent December 2025 letters reinstated NDI status. We can market NMN as a dietary supplement; we cannot make disease treatment claims. All claims should be framed as structure/function or "research shows" language.

### Cessation rebound (retention lever)
Per Katayoshi et al. (2023), blood NAD+ levels return to baseline approximately 4 weeks after stopping NMN supplementation. This is both:
- A **retention message** for at-risk subscribers ("Your NAD+ levels likely returned to baseline within 4 weeks of stopping")
- A **clinically accurate** statement that doesn't require any individual outcome claim

This is the single most defensible churn-intervention message available to us for NAD+. It should anchor the win-back sequence.

### What the evidence supports vs. what it doesn't (NAD+ specifically)

| We can defensibly say | We cannot defensibly say |
|---|---|
| NAD+ levels in the blood rise with NMN supplementation, with significant elevation typically observed by week 4 | "You will feel more energy by week 4" |
| Clinical research has studied NAD+ supplementation in relation to sleep, aerobic capacity, insulin sensitivity, and biological age | "NMN improves your sleep" or "NMN reverses aging" |
| NMN is safe and well-tolerated at recommended doses | Any disease treatment claim |
| In one RCT, biological age (Aging.AI 3.0) remained stable in supplemented groups while increasing in placebo over 60 days | "NMN will lower your biological age" |
| NAD+ levels return to baseline approximately 4 weeks after cessation | "Stopping is dangerous" or fear-based framing |

### Provider involvement points (NAD+)
- **Day 0–1:** Dr. Nabity-authored welcome letter in package. Establishes the clinical relationship from the first touchpoint.
- **Week 4 Clinical milestone:** Optional async or live provider check-in. The first real clinical interaction. This is the highest-leverage provider moment.
- **Month 3 Provider perspective:** Dr. Nabity AMA, his authored content, and an optional 1:1 consult upgrade. This is where the provider relationship is sold.
- **Month 4 Deeper science:** Biological age testing with provider interpretation. The test result is the conversation starter; the provider interprets, not the dashboard.
- **Month 6 Anniversary:** Provider-led annual review. Premium tier benefit. Bridge to peptides for eligible customers.

### Key clinical references by touchpoint

The references below should be cited in their respective touchpoints. Each is paired with the specific framing it supports — use this as the medical-review baseline.

- **Day 7 First week milestone:** Mechanism content references NMN → NAD+ biosynthesis. No specific trial citation needed; this is established biochemistry.
- **Week 2 Symptom awareness:** Kim et al. (2022) for the circadian/sleep connection. Framing: "research has studied," not "studies prove."
- **Week 4 Clinical milestone:** Yi et al. (2022) and Katayoshi et al. (2023) for the timing of NAD+ elevation. Frame as "clinical research shows NAD+ levels reach significant elevation around now."
- **Month 4 Deeper science:** Yi et al. (2022) Aging.AI 3.0 biological age finding. Frame as observational: "In one trial, biological age remained stable in supplemented groups while increasing in placebo."
- **Win-back sequence:** Katayoshi et al. (2023) cessation rebound finding.
