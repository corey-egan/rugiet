## Compliance Reminders

- **Never promise individual outcomes on a timeline.** Clinical evidence supports mechanisms (NAD+ levels rise, GH increases), not guaranteed personal results.
- **Optimization register only.** "Upgrade" and "capability" — never "fix" or "deficiency."
- **NAD+ is the only compound with a completed clinical evidence audit.** All other claims require audit before use in patient-facing content.
- **Provider conversations carry the individualization.** Marketing references trials in aggregate; providers contextualize for the patient.
