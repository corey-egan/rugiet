# Cognitive Modulator

> **Category overview:** Compounds targeting memory, attention, focus, and mental clarity through neurotransmitter modulation.
> **Compounds in this file:** Alpha-GPC, PE 22-28, Pinealon, N-Acetyl Selank, N-Acetyl Semax, Dihexa

---

## Category Description

Cognitive modulator drugs are medications designed to enhance or regulate cognitive functions such as memory, attention, focus, and mental clarity. These drugs target various neurotransmitters in the brain, such as dopamine, serotonin, and acetylcholine, which are responsible for cognitive processes.

One of the key benefits of cognitive modulator drugs is their ability to improve focus and attention, particularly in individuals with ADHD, enabling them to perform better in school, work, and daily tasks.

These drugs also help enhance memory and learning, which is beneficial for those with age-related cognitive decline or neurodegenerative conditions like Alzheimer's. By boosting cognitive performance, these medications can help individuals maintain independence and mental sharpness as they age.

In summary, cognitive modulator drugs play an important role in improving memory, focus, and mental performance, particularly for those with cognitive impairments, leading to enhanced productivity, independence, and well-being.

## Alpha-GPC

**Forms:** Nasal Spray

**Description:** Alpha-GPC (L-alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine) is a cholinergic compound derived from phosphatidylcholine, known for its cognitive-enhancing properties. It functions as a precursor to acetylcholine, a neurotransmitter crucial for memory, learning, and overall cognitive function. Clinically, Alpha-GPC is used to improve cognitive performance, particularly in conditions like Alzheimer's disease and dementia. It is also popular in sports medicine for its potential to enhance power output and recovery. Its ability to cross the blood-brain barrier effectively makes Alpha-GPC a valuable supplement for both cognitive health and physical performance.

**Suggested Dosing:** 18.75mg – 37.5mg daily

**Benefits of Alpha-GPC:**
1. **Enhanced Cognitive Function:** Alpha-GPC is reputed to improve memory, thinking skills, and learning. It is particularly popular among students and professionals looking to enhance mental performance.
2. **Increased Acetylcholine Levels:** By providing choline to the brain, Alpha-GPC supports the production of acetylcholine, essential for various brain functions, including memory formation and muscle control.
3. **Neuroprotective Properties:** Some studies suggest that Alpha-GPC can help maintain brain health, potentially providing benefits in neurodegenerative conditions such as Alzheimer's disease and dementia. It may help mitigate the cognitive decline associated with these conditions.
4. **Supports Athletic Performance:** Alpha-GPC has been shown to increase power output in athletes, possibly due to its role in facilitating neurotransmission and enhancing growth hormone production.
5. **Improvement in Stroke Recovery:** There is evidence that Alpha-GPC can aid in the recovery of brain function after an ischemic stroke when used as part of early treatment.
6. **Mood Regulation:** The increase in cholinergic activity might also help with mood stabilization, potentially alleviating symptoms of mood disorders.

Dihexa is primarily researched for its potential in treating neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's disease and for cognitive enhancement in healthy individuals. Its ability to cross the blood-brain barrier and directly stimulate brain cell growth makes Dihexa a promising candidate for improving memory, learning, and overall cognitive function.

**Suggested Dosing:**
- Oral: 5mg to 40mg daily
- Nasal: 1.25mg to 2.5mg daily

3. **Antidementia Properties:** Dihexa's potential to potentiate hepatocyte growth factor (HGF) and its receptor, c-Met, suggests that it could counteract the synaptic degradation seen in dementia, potentially slowing or reversing cognitive decline.
4. **Manage Depression:** By improving neural connectivity and potentially enhancing mood-regulating neurotransmitter functions, Dihexa may offer new avenues for managing symptoms of depression.
5. **Increases Social Intuition:** Improved cognitive processing could enhance the ability to read and respond to social cues, potentially increasing empathy and social intuition.
6. **Improves Memory & Creative Thinking:** Dihexa facilitates the growth and formation of synapses, crucial for memory retention and creativity, allowing for more innovative thinking and better recall of information.
7. **Improve Focus & Learning:** With enhanced synaptic connectivity, Dihexa can help sharpen focus and accelerate learning processes, making it easier to acquire new knowledge and skills.
