# Cellular Health

> **Category overview:** Compounds targeting energy production, oxidative stress reduction, and cell regeneration at the cellular level.
> **Compounds in this file:** NAD+, NMN

---

## Category Description

Cellular health drugs are medications designed to improve and maintain the function of cells, which are the building blocks of all tissues and organs in the body. These drugs target various aspects of cellular health, such as energy production, oxidative stress reduction, and cell regeneration. By optimizing cellular function, they help protect against diseases, slow aging processes, and enhance overall physical and mental well-being.

One of the primary benefits of cellular health drugs is their role in enhancing mitochondrial function, the part of the cell responsible for energy production. Cellular health drugs also focus on reducing oxidative stress, which can damage cells and contribute to aging and diseases like cancer, heart disease, and neurodegenerative disorders.

In summary, cellular health drugs support the body's basic functions at the cellular level, improving energy, reducing the risk of disease, and promoting faster recovery. These benefits contribute to better overall health, longevity, and quality of life.

## NAD+

**Forms:** Lyophilized Powder · Nasal Spray · SQ Injectable · Topical Cream

**Description:** NAD+, which stands for Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide, is a critical coenzyme found in every cell of the body, involved in various metabolic processes, including energy production, DNA repair, and cell signaling. As we age, NAD+ levels decline, leading to a decrease in cellular function and an increase in age-related diseases. Supplementing with NAD+ has been shown to enhance mitochondrial function, improve energy metabolism, and promote longevity. Clinically, NAD+ is used in anti-aging therapies, addiction recovery, and neurodegenerative disease treatment. Its role in maintaining cellular health and promoting longevity makes NAD+ a cornerstone in the field of regenerative medicine.

**Suggested Dosing:**
- IM or SQ: [100mg – 200mg] 3 – 5 days per week
- Nasal: 75mg – 150mg per day
- Topical: 25mg – 100mg per day

**Benefits of NAD+:**
1. **Boosts Energy Metabolism:** NAD+ is pivotal in the mitochondria, the energy powerhouses of cells, helping to convert food into the energy that powers cells.
2. **Promotes Cellular Repair:** NAD+ is involved in DNA repair and the maintenance of cellular health. It supports the activity of sirtuins, a family of proteins that regulate cellular health and aging, and PARPs (poly ADP-ribose polymerases), which repair damaged DNA.
3. **Regulates Circadian Rhythms:** NAD+ levels fluctuate throughout the day and influence the circadian clocks that govern daily physiological cycles. Maintaining robust NAD+ levels helps sustain a healthy sleep-wake cycle.
4. **Supports Brain Health:** NAD+ has been shown to benefit brain health by supporting the function of neurons and protecting against neurodegenerative diseases. It helps maintain mitochondrial function in brain cells, crucial for preventing cognitive decline.
5. **Anti-Aging Effects:** Enhancing NAD+ levels can stimulate the activity of sirtuins and other anti-aging molecules, potentially slowing down the aging process and extending lifespan.
6. **Enhances Weight Management:** By improving the efficiency of your metabolism, higher levels of NAD+ can help manage body weight and prevent metabolic disorders such as diabetes.

## Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN)

**Forms:** Capsule · Nasal Spray

**Description:** Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN) is a precursor to NAD+ and plays a crucial role in the body's energy production and metabolic processes. NMN is converted into NAD+ within cells, supporting mitochondrial function and enhancing overall cellular energy. As NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, NMN supplementation is believed to help restore youthful energy levels, improve metabolic health, and promote longevity. Clinically, NMN is being studied for its potential to combat age-related decline, improve insulin sensitivity, and enhance cognitive function. Its role in NAD+ biosynthesis makes NMN a promising candidate in the field of anti-aging and metabolic health.

**Suggested Dosing:** 300mg PO daily

**Benefits of NMN:**
1. **Supports Cellular Energy Production:** NMN boosts NAD+ levels, which are vital for energy production in cells, helping to improve overall energy levels and vitality.
2. **Enhances Mitochondrial Function:** By increasing NAD+, NMN supports mitochondrial health, the powerhouses of cells, leading to more efficient energy utilization and improved cellular function.
3. **Reduces Age-Related Decline:** NMN helps combat the natural decline in NAD+ levels that occurs with aging, supporting healthy aging and reducing the effects of age-related diseases.
4. **Supports Overall Metabolic Health:** NMN plays a role in maintaining healthy metabolism, aiding in processes such as fat breakdown, insulin sensitivity, and glucose regulation, which are important for metabolic health.
5. **Improves Cognitive Function:** By supporting mitochondrial function and energy production in the brain, NMN may improve cognitive performance, memory, and focus, especially as we age.
6. **Aids in Extending Lifespan:** NMN's ability to increase NAD+ levels and promote cellular repair and regeneration may contribute to longevity and extended lifespan in some animal studies, with potential for humans.
7. **Supports Overall Vitality & Mood:** NMN helps enhance mood and vitality by improving energy levels and metabolic function, contributing to a greater sense of well-being.
8. **Enhances Cellular Repair:** NMN supports the repair of damaged DNA and improves cellular regeneration, which is critical for maintaining healthy tissues and slowing the aging process.
