# Sexual Function

> **Category overview:** Compounds targeting sexual arousal, libido, and reproductive hormone regulation.
> **Compounds in this file:** PT-141 (Bremelanotide), Kisspeptin

---

## Category Description

Sexual function drugs are medications designed to address sexual dysfunction in both men and women, improving various aspects of sexual health and enhancing quality of life. These drugs are commonly used to treat conditions such as erectile dysfunction (ED), low libido, and other forms of sexual performance issues.

The benefits of sexual function drugs extend beyond the physical aspects of sexual health. These medications can improve emotional well-being by restoring intimacy and strengthening personal relationships, which can reduce anxiety, boost self-esteem, and improve mental health.

Overall, sexual function drugs contribute significantly to improving sexual health, emotional satisfaction, and overall quality of life by addressing both the physical and psychological aspects of sexual dysfunction.

## PT-141 (Bremelanotide)

**Forms:** Lyophilized Powder · Nasal Spray · SQ Injectable · Troche

**Description:** Bremelanotide, also known as PT-141, is a synthetic peptide known for its ability to enhance sexual arousal and treat sexual dysfunction. It works by activating melanocortin receptors in the brain, which are involved in sexual desire and arousal. PT-141 is primarily used to treat conditions like erectile dysfunction and female sexual arousal disorder. Unlike traditional erectile dysfunction medications, PT-141 acts on the central nervous system rather than directly on the vascular system, making it effective for both men and women. Its ability to enhance sexual desire and arousal makes PT-141 a unique and promising therapeutic agent in sexual medicine.

**Suggested Dosing:**
- SQ: 1mg SQ 60 minutes prior to sexual activity as needed.
- Nasal: 1-2 sprays each nostril 60 minutes prior to sexual activity as needed.
- Troche: 2-4mg prior to sexual activity as needed.

**Benefits of PT-141 (Bremelanotide):**
1. **Enhances Sexual Function:** PT-141 stimulates sexual desire and arousal by activating specific receptors in the brain that control sexual function, improving overall sexual response.
2. **Supports Libido:** It effectively increases libido in both men and women, making it beneficial for those experiencing low sexual desire.
3. **Improves Erectile Function:** PT-141 has been shown to enhance erectile function by promoting blood flow, helping men achieve and maintain erections more effectively.
4. **Assists in Sexual Dysfunction Treatment:** It is used to treat sexual dysfunction in both men and women, including conditions like erectile dysfunction (ED) in men and hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD) in women.
5. **Enhances Sexual Arousal:** PT-141 works directly on the central nervous system to boost sexual arousal, rather than just focusing on blood flow, like some other ED treatments.
6. **Supports Overall Sexual Health:** By improving sexual desire and performance, PT-141 enhances sexual well-being and overall satisfaction with sexual experiences.
7. **Aids in Managing Low Libido:** PT-141 is effective for individuals struggling with low libido, particularly those whose sexual desire has diminished due to hormonal imbalances, stress, or other factors.
8. **Improves Sexual Satisfaction:** By enhancing arousal and improving sexual function, PT-141 helps individuals achieve greater sexual satisfaction, improving relationships and quality of life.

## Kisspeptin

**Forms:** Lyophilized Powder · SQ Injectable

**Description:** Kisspeptin-10 is a decapeptide that plays a critical role in the regulation of the reproductive system by stimulating the release of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) from the hypothalamus. This action triggers the release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), which are essential for reproductive function. Clinically, Kisspeptin-10 is being investigated for its potential to treat reproductive disorders such as hypogonadism, infertility, and delayed puberty. Its ability to modulate the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis makes Kisspeptin-10 a promising therapeutic agent for restoring normal reproductive function.

**Suggested Dosing:** 100mcg – 200mcg SQ daily

**Benefits of Kisspeptin:**
1. **Supports Reproductive Health:** Kisspeptin stimulates the release of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH), which is essential for proper reproductive function and hormone regulation.
2. **Enhances Sexual Function:** By influencing the secretion of reproductive hormones like luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), kisspeptin supports enhanced sexual function in both men and women.
3. **Aids in Menstrual Cycle Regulation:** Kisspeptin helps regulate the menstrual cycle by ensuring proper hormonal balance, making it particularly useful for women experiencing irregular periods.
4. **Improves Fertility:** Kisspeptin plays a key role in the regulation of ovulation and sperm production, improving fertility in both men and women.
5. **Manages Hormonal Imbalances:** It helps to restore balance in the reproductive hormone system, aiding in conditions such as low testosterone in men or irregular hormone levels in women.
6. **Reduces Signs of Testicle Atrophy:** In men, kisspeptin can prevent or reverse testicular atrophy by supporting natural testosterone production and maintaining reproductive tissue health.
7. **Aids in Hypothalamic Disorders Treatment:** Kisspeptin is effective in treating hypothalamic disorders like hypothalamic amenorrhea.
8. **Enhances Libido:** Kisspeptin has been shown to enhance sexual desire and libido in both men and women by optimizing hormone levels.
